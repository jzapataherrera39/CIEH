V International Conference on Innovative Practices and Research in Foreign Language Teaching: ICTEFOL 2025

The 5th International Conference on Innovative Practices and Research in Foreign Language Teaching: ICTEFOL 2025 is a crucial event for the field of foreign language teaching. This conference, to be held in Montería, Córdoba, Colombia, will provide a dynamic space for researchers, educators, and practitioners to share and discuss the latest trends, research, and innovative practices in language teaching.

Event Description

The main objective of ICTEFOL 2025 is to foster the exchange of knowledge and experiences on how innovative practices and cutting-edge research are transforming the teaching and learning of foreign languages. Through keynote addresses, paper presentations, interactive workshops, and panel discussions, participants will be able to explore new methodologies, technological tools, and pedagogical approaches that are shaping the future of language education. The event seeks to inspire new ideas and collaborations that benefit both language teachers and learners, promoting excellence and inclusion in second language teaching.
